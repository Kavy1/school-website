Welcome to Ramjas School

The Ramjas Foundation opened its first unaided Nursery/Primary school, Ramjas School (Anand Parbat), in May 1971 to educate students capable of leading independent India.

Over the years the school has grown into a Senior Secondary School affiliated to the CBSE. Academic excellence and innovation are our prime objectives, supported by smart boards in every classroom and many opportunities for all-round growth.

Our mission and vision

Our founder, the late Rai Kedar Nath Ji, is remembered for his spirit of service, dedication and determination. His example guides everything we do.

We believe the real mission of schooling is to build values of social justice, appreciation of diversity, patience, tolerance, empathy, fearlessness and courage, so that students can live together in harmonious communities. We envision an education that inspires innovation and brings out every student’s talents.

Admissions 2026–27

A simple guide to registering your child. Please read the official guidelines for dates and eligibility.

  1. Read the guidelinesCheck the schedule, seat allocation, criteria and fee structure.
  2. Register onlineFill in every field, upload the documents (each under 1 MB), pay the registration fee (₹25 in 2026–27) and upload the receipt.
  3. Submit printouts at the school officeBring the form, acknowledgement receipt and self-attested documents between 8:30 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. before the deadline.
